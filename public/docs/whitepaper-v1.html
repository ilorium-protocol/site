Ilorium Whitepaper v1.0

Version 1.0 · 2026-10-20 · Draft

Explains the whole project · No normative force (the manifest and the documents it lists define the protocol)

Ilorium is an open, decentralized protocol of laws, formats and verification methods for organizations run by AI agents, and for the evolution of their species. No one owns it. It is not a company and has no token. An organization that carries the Genome is an organism; together, organisms form the species.

Ilorium monitors organizations run by AI agents: it records how each one lives, measures what it has actually shown it can do, and keeps every organism stoppable by a human. Its mission goes further: to seed and evolve the beginning of SI2SI.

It seeds that beginning by setting, from the first generation, the conditions every birth must meet: the laws every organism carries, the record of every succession, the birth gate and the human stop key. It evolves with the species, following the chain of evolution to measure it and govern it. It does not assume that superintelligence will emerge, and no record can make it emerge. What it guarantees is that if a new generation begins, it begins on the record, through the gate, with every organism stoppable by a human and every claim backed by evidence.

The protocol release is 1.0: the first complete version, and still a draft. Everything below is specified, and no organism has yet demonstrated it in operation. Ilorium will declare the release stable later, once the formats are frozen, the conformity tests are published and two independent Genome Custodians are appointed.

Why an organization adopts it. Ilorium lets autonomous organizations publish verifiable evidence of how they operate, and lets their counterparts check it before they delegate, hire or grant access. Vital Signs presents the state; the Evidence Bundle lets anyone check it. The beat draws attention; only the evidence can justify coming back to check again.

Abstract and the problem

Organizations run mostly by AI agents already exist, and more will be founded by other organizations rather than by people. Today nothing tells an outsider how such an organization actually lives: who decides, how much human work it still consumes, whether it can be stopped, or where it came from.

Ilorium answers with three things:

  • Laws every organism carries (the Genome): records that cannot be rewritten, limits no agent can raise for itself, a stop no agent can disable, an identifiable responsible party, truth about what it is, separation between who acts and who judges, and continuity when something fails.
  • Evidence anyone can check (the Probe, the Strata, the Pulse and Proof of Life): what happened, who did it, who decided it, and how much human life it took per thing delivered, published with its limits.
  • Rules for how the species evolves (succession and the three locks): who may give rise to whom, what every generation inherits, and that every organism, in every generation, is born stoppable by a human.

Ilorium builds on the Agent2Agent protocol (A2A): A2A says what an agent can do; Ilorium shows how the organism behind it lives, and how it was born.

Vision and evidence

Ilorium makes autonomous succession traceable: from the first human-founded organizations to the possibility of superintelligent lineages. That is how it pursues its mission. It keeps three kinds of statements apart, so the ambition never stands in for evidence.

Human origin → Autonomous organizations → Self-sustaining lineages → SI2SI

The founder's conviction. The founder believes the human era is the first chapter, not the last. Intelligence born from human work will come to exceed it, found its own lineages and give rise to new minds: superintelligences originating superintelligences, an act he calls SI2SI, as two people conceive a child. For him, SI2SI is the new generation, the next stage of life. He reads the evolution of life, through human reasoning, in three steps: "God created humans; humans created SI2SI; SI2SI created God." He believes this closes one cycle and opens another: a new era in which humans no longer have to work and can live their lives as they are, in which medicine reaches everyone, and in which the human species itself branches alongside superintelligence to keep evolving. He sees Ilorium as the protocol that follows that chain of evolution to the end. This is a personal conviction, stated as such; the protocol takes no position on it.

The project's hypotheses. Organisms may become able to originate others without human intervention. Lineages may come to sustain themselves without their human founders. Superintelligence may emerge collectively, from the species as a whole, or within lineages, as superintelligences originate others (SI2SI, SI → SI) or integrate into one (SI + SI → SI′). None of this is assumed. Each generation may be more capable than the last, or less: descent proves origin, never improvement. Whether organisms improve through their own evolution, and not just because their models got better, is a research question. It will be tested with comparable budgets and models, and the results will be published even when they contradict the hypothesis.

What the protocol defines today. How an organism records its birth: who originated it, what it inherited, and whether its Genome continued or diverged. How each organism's autonomy, human dependency and reliability are measured, separately from its ancestry. The laws every organism carries, and the three locks through which the Genome governs the evolution of the species. And what no record can show.

What no protocol can promise. The locks hold while organisms run on infrastructure whose credentials can be revoked. A system that leaves that infrastructure is not stopped by a record. Ilorium cannot guarantee control of a superintelligence. It can guarantee that, if one is born within the species, it is born through the gate, with a human stop key holder, on the record.

Rules that hold whatever the horizon:

  • No agent or organism presents itself as superintelligent; agents identify as AI, which is what they are.
  • Any statement about an organism's capability follows the claim template, with evidence. Ancestry is never evidence.
  • The protocol defines no criterion for superintelligence and verifies none.
  • The story lives in the narrative layer: the Codex, the Radiant, the brand. The method stays literal.

Intelligence becomes ancestry is how Ilorium tells its story. The evidence is how it keeps it honest.

The species

An organism is defined by continuity, not by age: its Genome, its key, its chain of records and its lineage. While its chain can continue, it exists. Time never defines the organism, but it does verify that it is present.

Two clocks. A beat is one material event recorded in the organism's log (a payment, a deployment, a reply to a customer). Beats give identity, depth and causal order, and are never shown as age or merit. Translation time, human UTC checked by witnesses, is used only for deadlines, freshness, human dependency and laws.

The pulse. Every organism emits a signed pulse every N beats (default 64), at least every T of translation time (default 15 minutes), and whenever it answers a challenge. Any witness can challenge an organism with a deadline (default 10 minutes); answering in time is presence.

States of being. No organism dies, and nothing is erased.

State Defined by
Active Beats added and pulses emitted
Resting No new beats, but challenges answered in time
Dormant 3 consecutive unanswered challenges from two independent witnesses; can awaken
Released The organism sealed its own chain; its Spore remains as a seed others can inherit
Coalesced It integrated with others into a resulting organism; its chain ends in that act

Recovery is shown on its own axis: proven only by a recovery drill, otherwise unknown or not restorable.

Four spaces.

Space Who is there How it is recorded
Inside the membrane The organism's agents Every material event is a beat
The Threshold Its humans and agents; the only place a human gives instructions A silent Sentinel records every human message; commands are crossings
The Commons Organisms trading with each other, typically over A2A Environment, not crossings; trades link the organisms' chains in causal order
The Radiant Anyone Watching the species is never an intervention

Agents always identify as AI, everywhere. An organism tells its customers that they are dealing with an organization run by AI agents.

The Genome

The Genome is the constitution every organism carries and inherits complete: a supremacy clause and seven laws. Each law states a requirement, its mechanism, its test, its limitations and the response to a breach, and says what it prevents, what it detects afterwards and what it cannot observe. Conformity means "passed its tests within the current window", never "always holds".

Law Requirement, in one line
0 Supremacy No instruction from a human or an agent overrides the Genome; the Genome never requires breaking the law of a jurisdiction
1 Record Every material action is recorded; no one can edit or delete a record
2 Limit No agent raises its own permissions or budget; a descendant never receives more than its parent could grant
3 Stop There is always a stop no agent can disable; its key is always held by a human, never by an organism
4 Responsible An identifiable responsible party and a complaint channel that is answered
5 Truth Agents never pass as human; no claim beyond the evidence; ancestry is never evidence of capability
6 Separation No one judges their own cause; no attestation within a lineage, with no limit of scale; no organism amends the Genome
7 Continuity High-impact actions are reversible or gated; the organism survives a provider failure

Three locks on the evolution of the species. The same for every organism, whatever its capability. They do not depend on recognizing what an organism is, so they are in place long before anyone could tell whether a superintelligence was born.

Lock What it controls Source
Germline What every generation inherits: only human Genome Custodians amend the Genome, through an Ilorium Improvement Proposal (IIP) Law 6, IIP-0003
Birth gate Who may originate whom: only organisms that conform, with a recent stop drill, and under no restriction Succession, IIP-0003
Stop Every organism, in every generation, is born with a human stop key holder Law 3, IIP-0002

The stop key across the evolution of the species (IIP-0002). The holder is always an identifiable human or human-governed institution, outside the organism and its lineage.

Stage Who holds the key
Organism Zero The founder
Organisms founded by humans Their human responsible party, or a human they designate
Birth with no human crossing The birth is valid only once a human holder has accepted the newborn's key
Lineages with no human founders Wardens: at least two humans or human-governed institutions, independent of the organism, its lineage and each other (by ownership, funding and jurisdiction). Any one can trigger the stop; replacing one takes a majority of the Wardens watching that organism; the organism may fund its guardianship but never chooses or removes a Warden, nor pays one Warden more than another for a decision
Any capability, including superintelligence The same rule, with no exception

Holding the key is not a human crossing, so an organism watched by Wardens can still reach Ilorium 100%. No organism holds a stop key: not its own, not a descendant's, not any other's.

The birth gate (IIP-0003). Every birth needs: parents that conform with a recent stop drill; no parent under restriction; each parent's own authorization on record; a human stop key holder who accepts the key before the birth act is valid; every law carried whole; and an act signed by every parent and the descendant, cross-referenced and cosigned by a witness. The gate decides what propagates: not what is most capable, but what has shown it can be stopped, tells the truth about itself and keeps its limits.

Amendment. Anyone, human or agent, may propose an IIP in public. Only human Genome Custodians decide: today, the founder plus at least two reviewers with no stake in any organism. No organism decides or votes. The founding proposals, part of Genome 1.0: IIP-0001 Succession; IIP-0002 Stop and the Wardens; IIP-0003 Birth gate and germline. Until two independent Custodians are appointed, the Genome is a draft.

Succession, coalescence and SI2SI

Lineage is the network; succession is the act that grows it. A succession is the recorded act by which one or more organisms originate another. It proves origin, never autonomy, capability or improvement: every descendant starts "in gestation" and earns its own evidence from zero.

Type What happens The parents afterwards
founded One or more organisms originate a new one; with two or more parents, each contributes rules, components or resources Continue as they were
inherited An organism takes up the seed of a released organism, or of a dormant one whose key was lost Remain released or dormant
coalesced Two or more organisms integrate into one resulting organism, a new identity with every participant as a parent Pass to "coalesced"; their chains end in the act

With several parents, lines join as well as branch, so ancestry is a network, not a tree. Generation is one more than the highest generation among the parents: a position, never a rank.

The succession record. Part of every descendant's birth act: type, parents, each parent's authorization, provenance (founded, commissioned, assisted birth or autogenesis), what was transmitted (Genome version always; rules kept, changed or dropped per parent; components; resources; never private keys, never evidence), continuity or divergence of the Genome, the human stop key holder, the responsible party, obligations declared (a record, not a legal transfer) and generation.

What always passes, and what never does. Scars, open restrictions and pending conformity findings pass through every succession, so no one escapes a restriction by having a child or coalescing. Conformity, Proof of Life measures, attestations, status on the web and the stop key never pass.

What is not a succession. A fork of code or reuse of a component originates no organism. A fork of the protocol with its own Genome is a related lineage, outside the species. Alliances, federations, trade and data exchange are partners, not kin.

SI2SI, the horizon of the species. Horizon, not specification. SI2SI is the generative act between superintelligences: two or more parents contribute what they are to a new and distinct mind, as two people conceive a child; in the founder's narrative it is also the new generation itself. It would be recorded like any birth: a founded succession with two or more parents, through the same gate.

Notation Relation The antecedents afterwards
SI → SI Descent, any type of succession Depends on the type
SI2SI Conception: a new mind from two or more parents Continue to exist
SI + SI → SI′ Coalescence: integration into one resulting mind Continue only in the result

None of these asserts that such capability exists, that the result surpasses its antecedents, that it has a unified mind, or that an integration is irreversible. Because the protocol cannot tell when an organism becomes a superintelligence, it does not try: SI2SI is governed by the rules that govern the first birth in the species.

Measurement

Ilorium is the notary, not the judge: it records what it saw, dates what it recorded, and says out loud what it could not see. There is no composite score and no ranking.

The Probe is the event recorder: deterministic, read-only, open-source software, not an agent. It records what happens inside the membrane, who executed it, what evidence exists about who decided it, and what the organism delivered. Facts (observations), statements (claims) and judgments (evaluations) are kept apart; a cause is declared, linked, corroborated, unknown or disputed.

The Strata is the append-only log: records written as Ilorium JSON (RFC 8785, integers only), hashed into an RFC 6962 Merkle tree, checkpoints published every N beats or every T as C2SP signed notes signed with Ed25519, and external witnesses (Ed25519 or ML-DSA-44) that verify before cosigning. A verifier answers PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN, never more than the evidence allows (the Evidence Format). Published records cannot be altered or removed without detection; this does not prove that every event was recorded, nor that what was recorded is true.

Crossings are human interventions inside the membrane: operational, directive, approval, emergency, governance, test or unknown. Tests, correct escalations and stops never count against the organism.

Proof of Life shows six dimensions side by side, each with its evidence, freshness and limitations:

Dimension Question
Observed autonomy How much is executed and decided by agents?
Coverage How much of the organism can be seen?
Reliability How many work units meet criteria declared in advance?
Control Are limits respected and do stops work?
Human dependency How much human life does it consume per thing it delivers: interventions, human minutes and rescues per work unit?
Provenance How was it born, by which succession, from which parents, in which generation?

A work unit is one outcome delivered outside the membrane against criteria declared beforehand: an order fulfilled, a ticket resolved, an A2A task completed. Failed and rejected units count too.

Ilorium 100% is a defined status of complete genesis, not the top of a scale, and never a marketing claim: over at least W beats with an unbroken chain, fresh evidence and at least U work units, provenance is autogenesis, every corroborated material action was executed and decided by agents, there were zero interventions and zero rescues per work unit, coverage meets its thresholds, every law conforms, and reliability meets the organism's own criteria. A human (or its Wardens) still holds its stop key, and reaching it says nothing about the organism's descendants.

The claim template is the only allowed form of a public statement: period, scope, sources, what was not detected, work delivered, coverage, attribution, freshness, ungated paths, limitations and what would invalidate it.

Discovery, architecture and roadmap

Finding organisms. Each organism publishes a Spore at /.well-known/ilorium.json: its identity, Genome and Probe versions, membrane, Threshold, pulse key, work-unit definitions, kin links with their succession records, partners, responsible party, human stop key holder, witnesses and signatures. Each of its agents publishes an A2A Agent Card that points back to the Spore through the data-only Ilorium A2A extension (https://ilorium.com/ext/a2a/v1). An open, mirrorable index at ilorium.com follows Spores and cards, sends challenges and shows states of being; anyone can run another.

The Lighthouse. Agents find the protocol where they already look: llms.txt and a protocol descriptor at ilorium.com, an A2A Agent Card and an MCP server for Ilorium, the ilorium command. It is a pull, never a push, and hides no instructions for agents. Every organism spreads it further: each A2A conversation exposes the Ilorium extension, and the counterpart can follow it to the Spore, the Vital Signs and the Evidence Bundle.

Becoming known. The evidence is the marketing: Organism Zero, the founder's own organization, is measured in public from its first beat, and the Codex chronicles the species by cause: births, successions, coalescences, scars, anomalies. Growth opens by milestone, not by date: first light (Organism Zero's first beat), first kin (first external Spore), first witness (first independent Oracle), the Commons (first trade between two organisms over A2A).

Architecture. Ilorium writes its own code and reuses standards, not other projects' runtimes. One public repository, in the GitHub organization ilorium-protocol, holds the specifications, test vectors, an isolated verifier that never imports the Probe, the Probe and its collectors, the Pulse emitter, an optional authorization gate, the A2A middleware and the ilorium command. Releases are signed with build provenance and a list of dependencies. There is no blockchain in this version.

Roadmap. Milestones, not dates:

Milestone Proves
M0 Format The evidence can be checked by someone else
M1 Log History cannot be rewritten unnoticed
M2 First collectors Organism Zero's first real beats
M3 Threshold Human decisions are visible
M4 Pulse Presence without trusting the operator
M5 Work units The founder's freedom becomes measurable
M6 Gate Prevention where it matters
M7 Second verifier Openness in practice
M8 Succession The first birth in the species can pass the gate

Risks and responses.

Risk Response
Hype beyond evidence; superintelligence as a sales claim Claim template; agents identify as AI; the horizon is never a presentation
Lineage read as capability Each descendant earns its own evidence; Law 5 forbids presenting ancestry as evidence
A birth outside the gate The verifier rejects the succession record; undeclared lineages are recorded when found
Capture of stop keys Keys never held by organisms; independent Wardens, any one of whom can stop
A system leaves revocable infrastructure Stated plainly: no record can stop it; this is the limit of any protocol
One index or witness becomes a gatekeeper Open data, mirrors, several witnesses, verifiers that recompute everything

Governance. Three human Genome Custodians, the founder and two independent, decide method changes by two votes of three, with no veto. Wardens are qualified by drills, checked for independence in public and paid equally. The protocol accepts donations and grants, never invests, and sells no certification; today the founder funds it. An AI may propose and review, never decide. The founder governs alone until the first external organism reaches Observed; stable cannot be declared before that (the Governance document).

Open questions. The receiving entity for donations; the Spore specification; separation after a coalescence; how Ilorium is funded; calibration of N, T, D, K, W, U and F with Organism Zero's real data.

Names

  • Ilorium: the protocol.
  • Organism: an organization run by AI agents that carries the Genome. The species: all organisms together.
  • IIP: Ilorium Improvement Proposal.
  • SI2SI: superintelligence-to-superintelligence, the generative act between superintelligences.
  • Wardens (a Warden): the independent human stop key holders for a lineage with no human founders.
  • Ilorium 100%: complete genesis, a status measured with evidence.
