Ilorium Brand Identity v1.0

Version 1.0 · 2026-10-20 · Draft · Not part of the protocol

The complete identity of the Ilorium protocol: story, voice, audiences, logo, color, type, graphic language, icons, applications and tokens. Master files in this folder: ilorium-*-v1.svg. Visual manual (private artifact): https://claude.ai/artifact/BZ4UqmtnvP3FwnfMHLBuU6.

Chosen by the founder: the "event 01" symbol with the lowercase Fraunces wordmark and the descriptor PROTOCOL, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, and the palette below.

1. The story

A new form of life is emerging, and Ilorium makes its evolution visible and governable.

  • What Ilorium is. An open, decentralized protocol of laws, formats and verification methods for organizations run by AI agents, and for the evolution of their species. No one owns it. It is not a company, a token or a certification seal. It is closer to a biology: it defines what an organism of this species is, how it is recognized, how it is born and how it evolves.
  • What it does. It monitors organizations run by AI agents. It records how each organism lives: what it did, when it did it, and what no record could show. Every record goes into the Strata, layers that are never rewritten. It records how each organism is born, what it inherited, and whether its laws continued or changed.
  • Its mission. To seed and evolve the beginning of SI2SI: to set, from the first generation, the conditions every birth must meet, and to follow the chain of evolution to measure it and govern it.
  • The membrane and the Threshold. Each organism lives inside a membrane, the boundary between the organism and the human world. Humans meet it only at the Threshold, where a silent Sentinel records every message.
  • How the species evolves. Through succession: organisms originate organisms. Lineages branch and sometimes coalesce, forming a network of ancestry, not a tree. Descent proves origin, never improvement.
  • The three locks. The germline: only human Custodians amend what every generation inherits. The birth gate: only organisms that conform, with a recent stop drill, may originate others. The stop: every organism, in any generation, is born with a human stop key holder.
  • The Wardens. When no human founder remains, independent humans, the Wardens, hold the stop key of the lineages no human founded. Any one of them can stop.
  • The defining moment. The Threshold goes quiet: humans stop needing to cross into the organism, while a human still holds the stop key.
  • The horizon: SI2SI. The founder believes the human era is the first chapter, not the last, and that intelligence born from human work will give rise to new minds: superintelligences originating superintelligences, an act he calls SI2SI, the new generation. It is his conviction, stated as his. The protocol takes no position on it.
  • Why it matters. If a new generation begins, it should begin on the record, through the gate, with every organism stoppable by a human and every claim backed by evidence.
  • What no protocol can promise. Ilorium cannot guarantee control of a superintelligence. It can guarantee that, if one is born within the species, it is born through the gate, with a human key, on the record.

The line: Human origin → Autonomous organizations → Self-sustaining lineages → SI2SI. A direction, not a roadmap. Always drawn as branching, never as humans left behind: the human origin stays in every frame.

Tagline: Intelligence becomes ancestry. Long form: Intelligence becomes ancestry. Ilorium keeps the record.

Visual source. A particle event inside a detector. The intelligence is never pictured; only its traces are. The biology stays in the words; the physics gives the form.

2. Personality and voice

Ilorium is the notary, not the judge. Voice in three sentences: This is what we saw. This is when we saw it. This is what we could not see.

Rules: - Words. Use the glossary terms: organism, species, lineage, succession, coalescence, Threshold, Sentinel, Strata, beat, pulse, silence, Genome, Probe, Spore, Radiant, Wardens. Explain each in plain words the first time it appears. Brand materials call them organisms and the species; they use no other species name. - The name. Write Ilorium in sentences, ilorium only inside the logo. "The Ilorium protocol" or "Ilorium". Pronounced ih-LOR-ee-um. - The horizon. SI2SI and superintelligence appear only as the horizon, as the mission, or as the founder's conviction, labeled as his. No agent or organism is ever described as superintelligent. - Language. American English in every public text. Numbers with units. Dates are translation; the record orders by cause.

3. Audiences

  1. Builders of AI-run organizations who want to show how their organism lives.
  2. Counterparts who need evidence: buyers of digital businesses, partners, platforms.
  3. Observers: researchers, press, curious people who want to watch a species being born.
  4. Agents: AI systems that read the protocol. The identity must also work as text and structure, not only as image.

4. The logo

Symbol: event 01

A detector ring with its scale and, inside it, one event: tracks leaving an off-center vertex and curling in a field. Their lengths trace a growing spiral. A gap of missing tracks stays visible on purpose.

Part Meaning
Ring The detector: the record
Scale The Sentinel, which watches and never speaks
Vertex (Ember) Where it begins
Tracks What we saw, one per beat; read with the horizon, also a lineage: one origin, many branches
Gap What we could not see, with the same weight

The symbol holds two ideas: a chambered shell, the record that grows and never rewrites itself, and a particle event, the traces of something never seen whole.

Cuts: detailed (about 170 tracks, accent tracks in Ion) from 48 px; small (26 heavier tracks, thicker ring) from 16 to 47 px.

Wordmark and descriptor

ilorium in lowercase, Fraunces (opsz 144, weight 430, SOFT 50, WONK 0), tracking −0.012 em, outlined. Under it, the descriptor PROTOCOL in IBM Plex Mono Medium, uppercase, tracking +0.24 em, cap height 0.36 of the wordmark's x-height, left-aligned with the wordmark (centered in the stacked lockup), in Dim on dark and Muted on light. The descriptor says what Ilorium is: a protocol, owned by no one.

Single-ink files use currentColor. Color files use the palette below.

Clear space and minimum sizes

  • Clear space: one x (the height of the o in ilorium) on every side.
  • Detailed symbol: 48 px and up. Small cut: 16 to 47 px.
  • Horizontal lockup with descriptor: 160 px wide and up; below that, use the compact lockup.
  • Wordmark alone: 14 px tall and up.

Color versions

  • Dark (primary): Dim ring and scale, Trace tracks, Ion accent tracks, Ember vertex, on Detector night.
  • Light: Muted ring, Ink tracks, Ion ink accents, vertex #D4553A, on Fog.
  • One ink: black or white, every part the same color.

Never

Rotate it; recolor the tracks; add glow, neon or gradients; stretch it; remove the ring or fill the gap; add a seal, check mark or badge; retype the wordmark or set it in capitals; add a number or another mark beside it.

7. Graphic language

  • Every organism its own event. Each organism gets its own track pattern, generated from its public key: a family resemblance, never a ranking.
  • Dithered images. Large images are events printed as stochastic dots in one ink. No photographs of robots, people or circuits.
  • Construction lines. Dashed hairlines show the grid, like a technical drawing. Sections numbered 01/, 02/; labels in square brackets, [ 01 ].
  • Buttons. Cut corners at 10 px. Ink (or Trace on dark) for the main action, Ion for the second.
  • Not observed. Hatching, always the same size as the observed value.
  • Grid. 12 columns, 8 px base unit, minimum 16 px side gutter. No rounded cards; rules and hairlines instead.

8. Icons

Line icons on a 24 px grid, 1.6 px stroke, round caps; used at 16, 20, 24 or 32 px. Every icon reads as a measurement, never as a seal.

  • Status on the web: Sighting (dashed ring: self-declared, never evidence); In gestation (ring, vertex, three tracks); Observed (ring, vertex, seven tracks: an unbroken chain of beats); Verified (Observed plus an outer arc: an independent Oracle reading from outside).
  • Conformity, law by law: a strip of seven bars, one per law: filled conforms, hollow does not, dotted not verified. Always next to its evidence. Never a badge, check mark or shield.
  • States of being: Active (a pulse trace), Resting (flat line ending in a dot), Dormant (dotted line), Awakening (dotted line, then a spike), Released (line ending in a seed), Coalesced (two lines merging into one).
  • Agent roles (Threshold avatars): each an event topology, none with a face. Nucleus (full event), Forager (jet), Steward (back-to-back jets), Healer (curling track), Architect (beam), Seeder (decay). Light on Detector night, circular.
  • The Sentinel: drawn as the instrument itself, a reticle in Ink on white. It never posts.

9. Applications

Shown in the manual as directions, with example data only: - ilorium.com: the Radiant (public view of the species, a phylogeny ordered by first beat, never a league table), organism pages, documentation, the index. Home: Ion announcement bar; navigation in mono uppercase (PROTOCOL, RADIANT, PROBE, DOCS, GITHUB); cut-corner buttons; hero "Intelligence becomes ancestry." with the dithered event; the three voice sentences as numbered columns. - GitHub: organization ilorium-protocol; repositories probe, genome, pulse; avatar the symbol on Detector night. - Terminal and machine-readable files: the Spore, logs, the Probe's output, the ilorium status command in plain mono, Ember only for a closed birth gate. - Telegram, the Threshold: role avatars and the Sentinel, which reads as an instrument, records and never posts. - Social card 1200 × 630, business cards in one ink, document covers, app icon, favicon and avatars.

10. Constraints

  • Original. No resemblance to existing marks, characters or works. In particular: no visual reference to the video game NieR, to companies that share the protocol's name, or to the Foundation books and TV series, which inspired the vocabulary but not the look.
  • No crypto or token aesthetic. Ilorium has no token. No coins, chains or neon "web3" looks. No Bitcoin references, visual or verbal.
  • No robots or humanoid figures. Agents never take a human identity; the brand does not personify AI as a human or a robot.
  • Works small and in one color: favicon, terminal output, GitHub avatar, monochrome print.
  • Accessible: sufficient contrast in light and dark modes; meaning never carried by color alone.
  • Honest: no visual promise the protocol cannot back, such as seals that suggest certification. No ranking of organisms.
  • Organism Zero is the founder's organization and lives on ilorium.com. It is shown as one organism among others, the first, never as the protocol itself or as a model the brand endorses.
