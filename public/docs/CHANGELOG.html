1.0 · 2026-10-20 · Draft

The first complete version of the Ilorium protocol. Every document starts at version 1.0. Release 1.0 is complete, not stable: it will be declared stable once its formats are frozen, conformity tests are published, a compatibility policy is written and two independent Genome Custodians are appointed.

Revision of 2026-10-21 (still draft). - governance-v1.md (new, part of the protocol): roles (founder, Genome Custodians, maintainers, Wardens, witnesses and Oracles, contributors) and stakes; kinds of change and the IIP process (30-day comment period for the Genome and governance, 14 for other method changes; two votes of three, no veto; public objection within 14 days); releases and what stable requires; three Custodians (the founder and two independent), appointed with a 30-day public notice, serving until resignation, with an independence review every two years and removal by the other two; Wardens' qualification, independence check, equal payment through a channel the organism cannot change, and what happens when all become unavailable (the organism is unwatched, its birth gate closes, interim Wardens); funding (donations and grants with a public rule of use; the protocol never invests in organizations; no sales of certifications; a receiving entity before any donation; conflict-of-interest rules; today the founder funds it); what an AI may do (propose, review, maintain, operate; never vote, hold a key, appoint or move money; extensions only by IIP), with the founder's hypothesis labeled as his; interim governance ends when the first external organism reaches Observed. - genome/genome-v1.md: the Amendment section points to Governance for how Custodians are appointed and replaced; the two open questions on Wardens and Custodians move there. - manifest.md: Governance row; governance in the layer table; "Who publishes this file" now follows the governance document. README.md: Governance row. - Decisions of the founder, 2026-10-21 (governance): three Custodians with the founder as one, two of three, no veto; indefinite term until resignation; interim governance until the first external Observed organism; AI proposes and reviews but never decides; donations and grants yes, investments never. - specs/evidence-format-v1.md: new section 12 (the Spore, with stop_key_holders), section 13 (the Evidence Bundle: a manifest over the Strata export and the Spore, no keys) and section 14 (Vital Signs, with no field for a score, a rank or a seal); the former sections 12 to 15 are now 15 to 18. A verifier without ML-DSA-44 reports those cosignatures as unsupported (UNKNOWN), never an error, and says so in the report; the reference verifier requires cryptography 48 or later. ML-DSA-44 vectors are made in the deterministic mode of FIPS 204, by an implementation independent from the verifier's, and regenerate byte for byte. Licensing section: AUTHORS, the DCO rule for agents, LICENSE-SPECS. - specs/probe-v1.md, specs/discovery-and-growth-v1.md: stop_key_holder is now stop_key_holders, a list of {kind: human|institution, id, name, contact, credential}; anyone on the list can stop the organism, so Wardens (at least two, a Genome rule) fit. This closes the disagreement between the two drafts. The Open items of Discovery and Growth now point to the Evidence Format for the Vital Signs schema and the bundle. - Code: schemas/ gains spore, bundle and vitalsigns; the verifier gains the commands spore, bundle and vitalsigns; vectors/ has 384 cases (306 before the bundle and Vital Signs; 288 in the first draft). Legal files: LICENSE-SPECS (CC BY 4.0 full text), AUTHORS, CONTRIBUTING.md with the DCO. - Decisions of the founder, 2026-10-21: cryptography 48 or later; deterministic ML-DSA-44 vectors; the stop_key_holders list with the rule "anyone can stop"; DCO, where commits by agents carry the Signed-off-by: of the responsible human or organization and the agent as author under its own identity; "The Ilorium Authors" kept, with an AUTHORS file; the full CC BY 4.0 text before the repository is published; the Evidence Bundle and Vital Signs defined before M1. The mission, stated for the first time in this release. Ilorium monitors organizations run by AI agents; its mission goes further: to seed and evolve the beginning of SI2SI.

Origins

Ilorium began in 2026 as a working draft called AUTOMATA, written by its founder with Claude. Drafts 0.1 to 0.4 were consolidated into release 1.0 and removed. Along the way the protocol took the name Ilorium; its organisms took the name "organisms" and the species "the species"; fusion became coalescence; the stop key was fixed in human hands, with Wardens for lineages no human founded; the birth gate and the human germline were added; and proposals were renamed Ilorium Improvement Proposals (IIP). The drafts have no authority.
